# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Laravel 13 + Inertia + React + TypeScript + Tailwind v4 (Laravel React starter kit, no auth). Separate project from the main TTE app, requested by the user.

## Users

Everyone in Makassar who might play padel or hang out at a padel venue: first-timers curious to try, community groups running mabar/Americano/trial-court sessions, regular players hunting a good court, and people who come for the cafe & resto. Most arrive on a phone, from Instagram/TikTok, a Reclub event link, or Google Maps.

## Product Purpose

Landing page for Rumah Kelinci Padel, a padel venue with cafe in central Makassar. Success = the visitor books a court (AYO) or chats the admin (WhatsApp), or decides to come by.

## Positioning

A padel club and cafe under one roof in the middle of Makassar: 4 international-standard courts plus the hospitality touches (welcome drink, cold towel, free mini gym warm-up area, wasit & ballboy on request) that make it feel like a house, not a rental hall. The name — "Rabbit House" — is the venue's own.

## Operating Context

- Booking runs through AYO Indonesia: https://ayo.co/v/rumah-kelinci-padel (no refund / non-cancellable per AYO listing).
- Community sessions are organised by third parties on Reclub (Americano, beginner, trial court).
- Instagram / TikTok: @rumahkelincipadel.
- Address: Jl. Gunung Bawakaraeng No.49, Gaddong, Kec. Bontoala, Kota Makassar, Sulawesi Selatan 90156. Coordinates -5.134867, 119.42111.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Facts from the AYO listing (Nov 2026):

- 4 international-standard padel courts.
- Price "mulai Rp150.000" per session, includes 2 boxes of 240 ml mineral water.
- Facilities: Cafe & Resto, hot shower, shower, toilet, changing room, musholla, car & motorbike parking, snacks & drinks, sports shop, Wi-Fi, gym & warm-up area (free mini gym), nursing room, wasit & score keeper, ballboy, racket rental, ball machine rental, welcome drink & cold face towel.
- Rating 4.9/5 (94 ratings, 22 reviews) on AYO; sub-scores cleanliness 4.80, court condition 4.78, communication 4.95.

Undecided / unknown — never invent: opening hours, WhatsApp number, per-slot pricing, indoor/outdoor, court surface. These live as clearly marked placeholders in one data file.

## Brand Commitments

Name: "Rumah Kelinci Padel" (also styled "Rumah Kelinci Padel & Cafe" on TikTok). No logo supplied yet. Copy in Bahasa Indonesia.

## Evidence on Hand

- AYO listing rating & facilities (above), quotable with source.

## Product Principles

1. Booking is one tap away from anywhere on the page.
2. Only verified facts; unknowns are placeholders, not guesses.
3. Sport and cafe are one experience, not two products.
4. Phone-first: most visitors come from social links.
